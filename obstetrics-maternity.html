About Obstetrics & Maternity Care at Dalawai

At Dalawai Maternity Hospital, our Obstetrics division is built around a single commitment: ensuring every mother experiences a safe, joyful, and dignity-filled birthing journey. Backed by over three decades of clinical expertise, our team provides complete maternal-fetal care starting from initial preconception planning to delivery and postpartum recovery.

We specialize in managing both seamless routine deliveries and complex high-risk pregnancy conditions, including gestational diabetes, preeclampsia, multiple gestations (twins), severe anemia, and prior C-section histories. Each pregnancy is monitored with personalized care plans, modern ultrasound scans, and continuous fetal wellbeing assessments.

Our maternity department features state-of-the-art labor and delivery suites, round-the-clock anesthesiologist availability for painless labor epidurals, and an immediate link to our Surgical Intensive Care Unit (SICU). Every mother receives continuous emotional and physical support from our senior obstetricians and trained birthing nurses.

COMPREHENSIVE CLINICAL SERVICES

Conditions & Procedures Treated

Full-spectrum obstetric care tailored for mother and newborn wellbeing.

01

Comprehensive Antenatal & Prenatal Screening

Routine trimester blood panels, genetic risk evaluations, gestational monitoring, and dietary guidance tailored for optimal fetal development.

02

High-Risk Pregnancy Management

Specialized clinical protocols for mothers facing gestational diabetes, preeclampsia, hypertension, thyroid disorders, and multiple gestations.

03

Painless Delivery Options (Labor Analgesia)

Round-the-clock epidural analgesia administered by expert anesthesiologists to minimize labor discomfort while keeping mothers alert and relaxed.

04

Normal & Assisted Vaginal Deliveries

Gentle, patient-centered birthing support utilizing evidence-based natural delivery techniques, perineal protection, and continuous monitoring.

05

Cesarean Section (C-Section) Deliveries

Planned and emergency surgical deliveries conducted in sterile, ultra-clean operating suites with rapid recovery protocols and minimal scarring.

06

Fetal Wellbeing & Doppler Scans

Advanced high-resolution ultrasonography to evaluate placental blood flow, amniotic fluid levels, organ anatomy, and intrauterine fetal growth.

07

Recurrent Pregnancy Loss Care

In-depth diagnostic evaluations, immunological screening, cervical incompetence management (cerclage), and proactive therapeutic support.

08

Postnatal Care & Lactation Counseling

Immediate postpartum monitoring, infant feeding guidance, wound care, emotional health support, and maternal physical recovery tracking.
