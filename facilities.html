STATE-OF-THE-ART INFRASTRUCTURE

Advanced Facilities
For Safer, Comfortable Care

At Dalawai Maternity Hospital, every physical facility is engineered for clinical precision, infection prevention, and patient serenity. From modular operating theatres equipped with positive-pressure laminar airflow to specialized critical care recovery units, our facility design places mother and child at the heart of advanced medical technology.

Whether assisting routine deliveries in private, calming labour suites or executing complex surgical interventions in our Surgical ICU (SICU), our round-the-clock emergency infrastructure guarantees immediate clinical readiness, 24/7 diagnostic support, and continuous medical supervision.

Surgical Suite

Modular Operation Theatre (OT)

Our ultra-modern, sterile operating theatre is designed for precision surgeries and optimal patient safety. Equipped with a state-of-the-art laparoscopic tower, advanced anesthesia workstations, and ensuring uninterrupted power supply for complex procedures. The laminar HEPA filtered airflow systems maintain a highly sterile environment to prevent any airborne bacterial exposure.

  • HEPA Filtered Airflow
  • Laparoscopic Tower
  • Anesthesia Workstations
  • Uninterrupted Power
Maternity Delivery

Private Labour Suites

Our private labour suites provide a tranquil, comforting environment, supporting both the physical and emotional wellbeing of mothers during labor. With constant fetal telemetry and ready epidural access, you can trust our maternal care specialists at every step. We also welcome companion support, assuring families are together through this beautiful journey.

  • Hygienic Environment
  • Fetal Telemetry
  • Epidural Access
  • Companion Support
Critical Care

Surgical ICU (SICU)

Our dedicated multi-bed Critical Care unit offers 1-on-1 expert nursing care for critical postoperative recovery. Comprehensive monitoring capabilities include advanced cardiac monitors and invasive ventilators, supervised 24/7 by experienced intensivists. Coupled with a centralized oxygen supply, we ensure swift, life-saving responses at a moment's notice.

  • Cardiac Monitors
  • Invasive Ventilators
  • 24/7 Intensivist
  • Centralized O2

PATIENT SAFETY & HYGIENE

Our Uncompromising Standards

Every process, every protocol, and every square metre of our hospital is governed by clinical-grade safety standards designed to protect patients, families, and clinical staff.

Infection Control

Our hospital follows a rigorous daily sterilization protocol across all wards, theatres, and recovery areas. Hospital-grade disinfectants approved for clinical use are applied after every procedure, and all surface touchpoints — beds, rails, switches, and instruments — are cleaned between patient contacts. Zero-contamination is enforced by our dedicated Infection Control Unit.

  • Daily full-theatre sterilization after every surgical slot
  • Dedicated Infection Control nurse assigned per unit
  • Glove, gown, and mask compliance actively monitored
  • Regular microbiological sampling and testing
  • Strict hand hygiene protocols for all staff

Clean Air & HEPA Filtration

Our modular operation theatres are equipped with laminar airflow systems and HEPA-grade air filters, removing 99.97% of airborne particles, bacteria, and contaminants. The positive-pressure environment ensures that unfiltered air from corridors never enters the surgical field — a non-negotiable standard for all gynaecological and laparoscopic procedures.

  • HEPA H14 air filtration in all operating theatres
  • Laminar airflow with positive-pressure control
  • Air quality audits conducted quarterly
  • UV-C disinfection systems in critical areas
  • Temperature and humidity monitoring 24/7

Uninterrupted Power & Oxygen

Critical care and surgical operations cannot tolerate a single moment of power failure. Our hospital maintains a dedicated emergency power generator that activates within milliseconds of any mains disruption, ensuring that ventilators, monitors, OT equipment, and NICU life-support systems continue without interruption. In parallel, a centralized medical oxygen supply — backed by reserve cylinders — guarantees uninterrupted respiratory support at every bed.

  • Millisecond-response backup generator system
  • Centralized piped medical oxygen to every ward
  • Reserve cylinder bank tested and replenished daily
  • UPS systems for critical medical equipment
  • Automatic power failure alarm systems

Infant Security

The safety of every newborn is secured through a multilayer surveillance and access-control framework. Our maternity wing operates under round-the-clock closed-circuit camera monitoring, and all entry points to the labour and neonatal areas are controlled through biometric verification. Every visitor undergoes identity checks, and our nursing staff perform regular scheduled infant security sweeps to ensure no infant is ever unsupervised.

  • 24/7 CCTV across all labour and neonatal zones
  • Biometric access control at all maternity entrances
  • Visitor identity verification at every ward entry
  • Mother-infant matching identification bands
  • Regular infant security sweeps by nursing staff

How Modern Hospital Facilities Connect to Ensure Patient Safety and Rapid Recovery

At Dalawai Maternity Hospital, patient safety is achieved through an interconnected healthcare continuum rather than isolated department units.
