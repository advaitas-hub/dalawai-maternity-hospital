About Outpatient (OPD) & Diagnostic Services

The Outpatient Department (OPD) at Dalawai Maternity Hospital serves as the primary entry point for comprehensive maternal health, gynecological evaluations, and wellness screenings. Designed for patient convenience and minimal wait times, our OPD runs daily consultation clinics staffed by senior specialists.

Our OPD is equipped with on-site high-resolution ultrasound machines, allowing real-time fetal scans, pelvic sonography, and immediate clinical evaluation during your consultation visit. Combined with our fully automated diagnostic laboratory, patients receive timely diagnostic answers without needing to visit outside centers.

Whether visiting for routine pregnancy check-ups, menstrual irregularities, fertility advice, preventive Pap smears, or senior women’s health evaluations, every patient receives unhurried, empathetic medical counseling in comfortable consultation suites.

OUTPATIENT CLINICS & DIAGNOSTICS

OPD Clinics & Diagnostic Services

Comprehensive outpatient services covering consultations, imaging, and lab tests.

01

Antenatal Routine & High-Risk Check-Ups

Regular trimester health tracking, blood pressure monitoring, fundal height measurement, and fetal wellbeing assessments.

02

High-Resolution Obstetric & Pelvic Ultrasound

Early viability scans, nuchal translucency (NT) scans, detailed anomaly scans, growth scans, and Doppler blood flow studies.

03

Gynecological Consultation & Screening

Expert diagnostic evaluations for pelvic pain, heavy menstrual bleeding, fibroids, ovarian cysts, and uterine disorders.

04

PCOS & Endocrine Health Clinic

Metabolic evaluations, hormonal blood testing, weight management counseling, and personalized menstrual regulation plans.

05

Fertility Evaluation & Pre-Conception Guidance

Follicular ultrasound tracking, ovulation study monitoring, semen analysis review, and initial fertility counseling.

06

Cervical Cancer Screening & Colposcopy

Routine liquid-based Pap smears, high-risk HPV DNA testing, cervical visual inspection, and colposcopic biopsy evaluations.

07

Full Blood Pathology Laboratory

On-site automated hematology, thyroid panels, blood sugar monitoring, urine chemistry, and infectious disease screening.

08

Menopause & Senior Women’s Wellness

Bone density assessment guidance, hormone replacement therapy discussions, cardiovascular risk screening, and breast health advice.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding OPD timings, walk-ins, and diagnostic scans.

Our OPD clinic operates Monday through Saturday, from 9:00 AM to 1:30 PM in the morning session, and 4:30 PM to 8:00 PM in the evening session. Emergency services are available 24/7.

While walk-ins are welcomed, booking an advance slot online or by phone ensures minimal waiting time upon your arrival at the hospital desk.

Yes. Our consultation rooms feature integrated ultrasound scanning equipment, allowing senior doctors to perform routine antenatal and pelvic scans immediately during your consultation.

Routine blood counts, blood sugar, and urine test results are processed within 1 to 2 hours in our automated in-house pathology lab. Specialized hormone or culture reports are delivered within 24 to 48 hours.
