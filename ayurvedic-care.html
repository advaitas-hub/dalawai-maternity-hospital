About Ayurvedic Women's Care & Sutika Paricharya

At Dalawai Maternity Hospital, our Ayurvedic division bridges ancient Vedic wellness wisdom with modern clinical obstetrics. Led by Dr. Jyothi Dalawai (BAMS), our department specializes in classical post-delivery care—known as Sutika Paricharya—which focuses on restoring uterine health, toning abdominal muscles, promoting lactation, and revitalizing maternal vitality.

The postnatal period (Sutika Kala) is a delicate transformation phase. Through therapeutic herbal oil massages (Abhyanga), warm medicated steam (Swedana), customized herbal decoctions (Kashayam & Arishta), and abdominal cloth wrapping (Udara Bandhana), we help new mothers regain their energy and emotional equilibrium naturally.

Beyond postnatal care, our clinic offers holistic herbal treatments for chronic gynecological concerns including PCOS, irregular menses, dysmenorrhea, menopausal symptoms, and metabolic stress. All treatments utilize authentic, quality-checked Ayurvedic preparations administered in a peaceful, hygienic therapeutic space.

Natural galactagogue formulations and herbal dietary therapies to improve breast milk supply, flow quality, and maternal nourishment.

PCOS & Hormonal Balance Management

Holistic herbal therapies, metabolic detox, and dietary adjustments to regularize menstrual cycles and reduce insulin resistance naturally.

Dysmenorrhea & Pelvic Pain Relief

Targeted anti-inflammatory herbal formulations and local warm oil therapies (Kati Basti) to relieve severe menstrual cramps and lower back ache.

Postpartum Weight & Abdominal Toning

Nourishing Rasayana herbs, cooling formulations, and lifestyle guidance to manage hot flashes, mood swings, and bone density changes.

08

Preconception Body Cleansing (Garbhadhana)

Mild gentle detox rituals, cellular nourishment, and reproductive tissue strengthening in preparation for a healthy pregnancy.
