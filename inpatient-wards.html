About Inpatient Wards & Private Suites

The Inpatient Department (IPD) at Dalawai Maternity Hospital is designed to give mothers, patients, and their families a comfortable, healing environment. We understand that a clean, quiet, and supportive room plays a vital role in post-delivery healing and surgical recovery.

We offer a variety of room categories ranging from air-conditioned Deluxe Private Suites to Semi-Private and General Wards. Every room is equipped with ergonomic patient beds, nurse call buttons, attached sanitized bathrooms, visitor seating, and continuous hot water supply.

Our dedicated nursing staff provides 24-hour bedside care, regular vital checks, timely medication administration, and coordinated physician rounds. Strict sanitation protocols and controlled entry policies ensure an environment free from disturbance and infection.
