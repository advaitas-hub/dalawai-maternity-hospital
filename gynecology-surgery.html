About Gynecology & Laparoscopic Surgery

The Department of Gynecology & Minimal Access Surgery at Dalawai Maternity Hospital is dedicated to diagnosing and treating a wide array of female reproductive disorders using modern keyhole surgical techniques. Our surgical suite is outfitted with HD laparoscopic cameras and advanced electrosurgical instrumentation, enabling supreme precision.

Minimally invasive laparoscopic surgery replaces traditional large abdominal incisions with 3 to 5 millimeter keyhole punctures. This offers significant medical benefits: reduced post-operative pain, lower infection risk, minimal blood loss, minimal tissue disruption, and a return to normal routine within days rather than weeks.

We manage benign gynecological conditions including uterine fibroids, ovarian cysts, endometriosis, pelvic organ prolapse, and abnormal uterine bleeding. Furthermore, our outpatient gynecological division provides compassionate consultation for PCOS, menopausal health, menstrual regulation, and preventive cervical cancer screening.

SURGICAL & CLINICAL SPECTRUM

Conditions & Procedures Treated

Minimally invasive and gynecological procedures performed by senior specialists.

01

Total Laparoscopic Hysterectomy (TLH)

Keyhole removal of the uterus for symptomatic fibroids, severe adenomyosis, or chronic bleeding with minimal tissue trauma and fast healing.

02

Laparoscopic Ovarian Cystectomy

Precise keyhole excision of dermoid, endometriotic, or simple ovarian cysts while preserving surrounding healthy ovarian tissue and fertility potential.

03

Laparoscopic Myomectomy (Fibroid Removal)

Minimally invasive removal of benign uterine fibroids while reconstructing the uterine wall to relieve heavy bleeding and preserve reproductive function.

04

Endometriosis Ablation & Adhesiolysis

Laparoscopic identification and excision of pelvic endometrial implants and scarring to alleviate severe pelvic pain and improve fertility outcomes.

05

Diagnostic & Operative Hysteroscopy

Direct camera evaluation of the uterine cavity to diagnose polyps, intrauterine septums, or adhesions and perform keyhole resection without abdominal cuts.

06

Pelvic Organ Prolapse & Incontinence Surgery

Surgical reconstruction and laparoscopic sacropexy to repair uterine/vaginal prolapse and restore pelvic support and bladder control.

07

PCOS & Menstrual Irregularity Management

Comprehensive hormonal evaluations, medical therapy, metabolic management, and laparoscopic ovarian drilling when non-surgical options fail.

08

Cervical Screening & Pap Smears

Routine liquid-based Pap cytology, HPV co-testing, colposcopic evaluation, and early cervical dysplasia treatment to prevent cervical cancer.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding keyhole surgery, recovery times, and benefits.

Laparoscopic surgery uses tiny 3-5mm incisions instead of a large abdominal incision. This results in far less post-operative pain, minimal scar visibility, reduced risk of wound infection, shorter hospital stay (often 24-48 hours), and a much quicker return to work.

Most patients are discharged within 24 to 48 hours following a TLH and can walk comfortably on the same day. Full return to light daily activities occurs within 1 to 2 weeks, compared to 6 to 8 weeks for open surgery.

Procedures such as laparoscopic cystectomy or myomectomy are specifically designed to preserve healthy uterine tissue and ovarian function. In fact, removing fibroids or treating endometriosis often significantly improves fertility outcomes.

Standard pre-op workup includes routine blood counts, coagulation tests, blood sugar evaluation, ECG, chest X-ray, pelvic ultrasound scan, and an anesthesia fitness evaluation.

Yes, medically necessary laparoscopic surgeries (such as hysterectomy, fibroid removal, and cystectomy) are standard covered procedures under most health insurance policies. Our hospital administrative desk provides complete pre-authorization support.
