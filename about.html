OUR LEGACY

A Legacy of Trust, Warmth & Healing

Founded with a vision to provide specialized, compassionate, and affordable healthcare for women and mothers, Dalawai Maternity Hospital has grown into a premiere center for obstetrics, gynecology, and surgical critical care.

We combine high-end medical technology with a personalized human connection. From antenatal monitoring and pain-free labor suites to specialized post-op recovery and integrative Ayurvedic care, our clinical teams prioritize patient comfort and safety above all else.
