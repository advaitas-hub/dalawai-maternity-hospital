Our Story

A New Beginning
for a Healthier Tomorrow

Dalawai Hospital was established on 13 August 2026 in Jamkhandi with a vision to provide accessible, ethical and quality healthcare to individuals and families in the community.

Led by Dr. Satish Dalwai, a dedicated Obstetrician & Gynecologist, the hospital places special emphasis on women’s health, maternity care and comprehensive gynecological services — delivered with compassion, integrity and personalized attention.

From the very first consultation to post-natal care, our team walks alongside every patient with warmth and expertise.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Two guiding principles that shape every decision, interaction and service at Dalawai Hospital.

Our Vision

To become a trusted centre for compassionate, ethical and quality healthcare in Jamkhandi and the surrounding communities.

We envision a healthcare environment where every patient can access dependable medical expertise, modern facilities and personalized care in a safe and respectful setting.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, patient-centred healthcare with compassion, integrity and clinical excellence.

  • Provide quality and ethical medical care to every patient.
  • Deliver specialized women's and maternity healthcare.
  • Maintain a safe, hygienic and patient-friendly environment.
  • Use appropriate modern medical practices and technology.
  • Support patients through clear communication and informed care.
  • Promote preventive healthcare and health awareness.
  • Continuously improve our services and standards of care.
